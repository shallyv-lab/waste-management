THE WASTE PROBLEM IN DANDORA

Poor waste disposal affects our environment, health and quality of life. Some communities face garbage heaps, dirty drainage and pollution.

Garbage Heaps

Uncollected rubbish makes neighbourhoods dirty.

Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste can pollute land and water.

Blocked Drainage

Waste in drains can contribute to flooding.

Health Risks

Poor waste management can create unhealthy surroundings.

OUR SOLUTIONS

We believe that a cleaner Dandora starts with responsible waste disposal and community action.

Waste Sorting

Separate plastic, paper, glass and organic waste.

Recycling

Reuse materials and reduce waste sent to dumpsites.

Community Cleanups

Encourage residents to participate in cleanup activities.

Environmental Education

Teach communities about proper waste disposal.
